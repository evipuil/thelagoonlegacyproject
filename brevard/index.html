Active work

Two Brevard projects, one lagoon focus.

Both projects center on the Indian River Lagoon and the choices Brevard residents make every day. Project: Save Our Lagoon focuses on classroom education and outreach. Project: BLACKOUT focuses on making the county fertilizer ordinance visible and easy to follow.

Project: Save Our Lagoon

Classrooms, experiments, and community outreach.

Student-led presentations, nitrogen tests, runoff demonstrations, posters, and online awareness for the Indian River Lagoon. The project won 3rd place at the Future Problem Solving International Conference in Community Problem Solving.
Project: BLACKOUT

Summer fertilizer ordinance activation.

Retail shelf tags, storm drain reminders, surveys, and public messaging around Brevard County's June 1 to September 30 blackout window.
