Active work

Our first projects.

Both projects focus on the Indian River Lagoon and everyday choices Brevard residents make. Project: Save Our Lagoon ran classroom lessons, nitrogen tests, and runoff demos at two elementary schools. Project: BLACKOUT places ordinance reminders near fertilizer displays and storm drains.

Project: Save Our Lagoon

Classroom lessons, nitrogen testing, runoff demos, and posters.

West Shore students taught lagoon science at two elementary schools, reached 200+ students, and placed 3rd internationally at Future Problem Solving.
Project: BLACKOUT

Brevard's June 1–September 30 fertilizer blackout campaign.

Retail shelf tags, storm drain reminders, resident surveys, and plain-language flyers about the county's summer fertilizer rule.
