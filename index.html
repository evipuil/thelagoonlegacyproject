Mission

Local projects, shared stewardship.

The Lagoon Legacy Project gives student teams a lasting place to publish conservation work, connect with local partners, and help residents understand the everyday choices that shape lagoon health.

The first chapter is in Brevard County, where two projects focus on the Indian River Lagoon from different angles: public education through Project: Save Our Lagoon and ordinance awareness through Project: BLACKOUT. Project: Save Our Lagoon won 3rd place at the Future Problem Solving International Conference in Community Problem Solving.
